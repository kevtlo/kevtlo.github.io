rclone-kev privacy policy

rclone-kev is a private OAuth client used by a single person, its owner, to access their own Google Drive with the rclone command-line tool.

It has no users other than its owner. It collects no data from anyone, shares nothing with any third party, and stores nothing anywhere except the owner's own computers and the owner's own Google Drive.

Google user data accessed through this client is used only to list, upload and download the owner's own files.
